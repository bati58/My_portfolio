Selam Beauty Salon

A salon mobile app UI/UX concept designed for onboarding, easy sign-in, friendly service browsing, stylist selection, and appointment booking with a warm, beauty-first visual tone.

Welcome screen

Opening visual that sets the salon mood before users move into discovery and booking.

Home dashboard

Personalized landing screen with a main booking CTA, service shortcuts, and featured stylists.

Sign in

Simple login flow with clear fields, remember-me support, and alternate Google sign-in.

Hair services

Category-based service browsing with pricing and duration shown before booking.

Facial category

Service cards focused on visual appeal while keeping selection and pricing easy to scan.

Stylist selection

Certified professionals stylist list with images, roles, ratings, and years of experience.

Booking progress

Multi-step appointment flow that clearly shows service, stylist, and scheduling progress.

Profile management

Clean account overview for personal info, service history, payments, preferences, and logout.

UI/UX Figma Mobile App Appointment Flow

  • Balances soft salon branding with clear task-focused mobile navigation.
  • Covers authentication, home, categories, stylist selection, and profile screens.
